## Notes

Golden Visa processing is priced per-person, so may apply only for Ben.
Once Golden Visa is processed, any linked dependent visas are automatically cancelled if not included in new visa.
For this reason it may be best to delay application until 1-month before Shabnam's departure.

**Health Insurance**
For Sept-Nov insurance, options appear to be Safetywing, Genki, or World Nomads insurance.
Genki is $260 AUD/mth for couple, Safetywing is $290 AUD/mth for couple, World Nomads is $445 AUD/mth for couple. TID (who may or may not accept mid-trip insurance) is $460 AUD/mth (but often has promos).
Genki gets better reviews, but Safetywing covers travel delays/cancellations as well as health incidents.
